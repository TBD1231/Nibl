## Decision Making Process: 

1. Propose: Anyone can raise an option with a brief rationale.
2. Discuss: Team weighs trade-offs together. Ask for advisor input if related to scope or direction.
3. Decide: Vote on the raised option. For ties, flip a coin.
4. Record: The documentation owner will note the decision and reasoning in the design notebook. 

## Conflict Resolution Escalation Plan: 

1. Address it directly: The teammates involved talk it through first, privately and promptly.
2. Bring it to the full team: If unresolved, raise it at the next team meeting for group discussion.
3. Escalate to your advisor: If still unresolved, ask the advisor to help mediate or clarify direction.
4. Involve the instructor: As a last resort, ask the course instructor to help mediate a persistent conflict.
